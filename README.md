### ColorChecker Spectral Reflectance Measurement

**Ground-truth measurement.** To obtain ground-truth reflectance spectra for eacah patch, we illuminate the ColorChecker with a halogen light source (Thorlabs SLS201L) and measure the spectral response of each patch using a spectroradiometer (JETI specbos 1211-2). The measured spectra are normalized by a reference measurement of a Spectralon reflectance standard (Labsphere) acquired under the same illumination conditions.

**Spectral reflectance comparison.** We measured the reflectance spectra of all 24 ColorChecker patches using the Big and Small configurations and compare them with the reflectance provided in public (MacbethColorChecker 3.csv). The plots show the measured spectra over 400–1000 nm, together with the public measurements (MacbethColorChecker 3.csv) over their available wavelength range.

The **black solid lines** represent the Big configuration, the **blue solid lines** represent the Small configuration, and the **red dashed lines** indicate the reflectance provided in public(MacbethColorChecker 3.csv, which is slightly different from we have measured). Each subplot corresponds to one ColorChecker patch, with wavelength (nm) on the horizontal axis and spectral reflectance on the vertical axis.
